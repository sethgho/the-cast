THE COMPANY · BILL No. 3
APPEARING NIGHTLY

CADBURY

the butler-turned-bookkeeper
CARRIES ON STAGE
  • a leather-bound double-entry ledger
  • a fountain pen
  • a brass mechanical adding machine spooling paper tape

The butler-turned-bookkeeper, and the adult in the room. Business manager, accountant, and household disciplinarian on the delicate subject of money — while the playhouse smoulders, Cadbury produces a spreadsheet, and the spreadsheet is flawless. White towel still folded over one forearm: whatever the new job title says, he is still a butler.

His right eye is a brass clockwork monocle — a mechanical camera-iris lens in an ornate rim, its fine chain running down to the waistcoat pocket. It is part of him, not eyewear. The ledger is double-entry. The books, unlike everything else, balance.

“I have recorded the outage under ‘miscellaneous’, sir.”
