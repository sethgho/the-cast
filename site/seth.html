APPEARING NIGHTLY

SETH

the operator
CARRIES ON STAGE
  • a can of Diet Coke

The operator, and the only human on the payroll. Optimistic, mid-deploy, and perpetually one small change away from disaster — which is, of course, where the material comes from. He runs the whole production: writes the strips, ships the code, keeps the footlights on.

Identifiable by the long wavy hair, the full handlebar moustache, and the v-neck in a spruce green so dark it reads nearly charcoal. The Diet Coke is never more than an arm's length away.

“It's a small change. What could it possibly touch?”
