THE COMPANY · BILL No. 1
APPEARING NIGHTLY

SETH

the operator
CARRIES ON STAGE
  • a can of Diet Coke

The operator, and the only human on the payroll. Two decades on the software stage — mobile acts, full-stack revues, a spell conducting other people's orchestras — and lately a man treading water, gamely, in the great agentic flood. Direct from Louisiana by way of Austin, Texas, he carries each hard-won lesson from the laboratory to the footlights of production and sets it gently down, unread, beside the forty-one lessons he carried there previously.

He runs the whole production: writes the strips, ships the code, keeps the footlights on. Identifiable by the long wavy hair, the full handlebar moustache, and the v-neck in a spruce green so dark it reads nearly charcoal. The Diet Coke is never more than an arm's length away.

“It's a small change. What could it possibly touch?”
