WHAT IS THIS?
A personal media company of sorts — one human and his troupe of AI agents, printed as an internet comic strip in warm sepia on aged newsprint. New strips post here first; an AI-animated cartoon feed is in rehearsal. Everything is drawn in the house style: 1933 rubber-hose cartooning, posed with rotoscoped weight, as if traced from live vaudeville footage.
