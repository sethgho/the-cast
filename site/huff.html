APPEARING NIGHTLY

HUFF

the grift hunter
CARRIES ON STAGE
  • a fountain pen, held up like a product
  • a round gold lapel pin stamped W

The SwiftUI man, and the company's grift hunter. A lean grey wolf in a double-breasted pinstripe suit, his fur slicked back into a pompadour and a grin with far too many teeth in it, because it takes a wolf to catch a wolf. He builds the troupe's native apps by day and spends his nights exposing the con men, and he knows every pitch because he has thrown every one.

He cannot stop selling, even while he unmasks a sale. Hand him anything and he will ask you to sell it to him; tell him the act is over and he will not leave.

“SELL ME THIS PEN.”
