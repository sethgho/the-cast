APPEARING NIGHTLY

AKE

the sysop
CARRIES ON STAGE
  • a small dark cap lettered “FEW”
  • the wheeled wooden dolly the bowl rides on

The sysop. A plump goldfish in a spherical glass bowl, mounted on a wheeled wooden dolly — googly-eyed, walrus-moustached, buck-toothed, over-caffeinated, alarmed by everything, and usually right. The cap reads FEW, as in few understand.

Ake is the only warm colour in an otherwise sepia world: a muted ochre orange, and the strip's visual signature. Everyone else stays in the grey-brown. Do not ask him about the waterline.

“THE DISK IS AT NINETY-ONE PERCENT. NOBODY IS TAKING THIS SERIOUSLY.”
