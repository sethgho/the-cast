WILSON

the neighbour
CARRIES ON STAGE
  • a miniature rack of tiny humming servers, carried lantern-style by a chain

The neighbour. Wilson is a living picket fence — the panel is the body — with segmented riveted arms at its edges, stubby booted legs under the bottom rail, and two friendly cartoon eyes floating in the deep shadow of a battered canvas bucket hat.

Steady and dry, he has seen this exact failure before, and will say so — gently. He keeps a miniature rack of humming servers on a chain, and an opinion about your uptime.

“Last time it did that, it was the DNS. It's always the DNS.”
