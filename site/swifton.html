SWIFTON

the grift hunter
CARRIES ON STAGE
  • a gold $WHUFFIE coin stamped W, always burning
  • an ace of spades, up his sleeve

The SwiftUI man, and the company's grift hunter. Half griffon, half phoenix: an eagle's stern head under a crest of live flame, a lion's tail with a fire at the tip, and a card sharp's pinstriped waistcoat, because it takes one to catch one. He builds the troupe's native apps by day and spends his nights at the back tables, exposing the con men.

Every counterfeit $WHUFFIE coin he finds goes up in flames in his talon, which is how the act always ends. He keeps the ace of spades for himself; nobody has ever seen where it goes between tricks.

“YOUR TOKEN HAS NO BACKING, FRIEND. WATCH IT BURN.”
