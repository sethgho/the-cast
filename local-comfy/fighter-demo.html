Seth, playable

Seven moves, each one a MiniMax H3 clip rendered on a magenta stage by the sprite-clip app and packed into a normalised atlas by sprite_sheet.py — all on gpu-worker, no cloud credits. Nothing here is video: it is seven PNGs and a state machine. Click the screen, then use the keys.

Why this works at all. Every cell is the same size and every frame is aligned on the bottom centre of the character's own silhouette, so the state machine can cut between moves mid-stride without him jumping around. That is the whole point of normalising: a sheet you can swap into a state machine, not a video you can only play.
