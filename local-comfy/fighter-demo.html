Seth, playable

Seven moves, each one a MiniMax H3 clip rendered on a magenta stage at 832×832, 20 steps — the character filling the frame — then keyed, cut to its pose extremes and packed into a normalised atlas. All on gpu-worker, no cloud credits. Click the screen, then use the keys.

The blur was draft settings, not the method. The first version rendered 512×512 at 10 steps, which draws the character about 240px tall in a soft frame — and then the demo magnified him 2.4×. At 832×832 and 20 steps he fills the frame at roughly 600px, so the packer is scaling down rather than up, which is where crispness comes from. Cost: 170s a move instead of 50.

Three things the packer does with those clips. It keys the magenta against the colour it measures in the frame rather than a constant; it aligns every cell on the bottom centre of the character's own silhouette, so his feet stay planted; and it nudges each pick to the sharpest frame within a couple either side, because H3 motion-blurs a limb that is travelling fast and the beat often lands on one of those.
